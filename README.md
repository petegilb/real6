# Real6

A stealth-puzzle game made in 48 hours for [Global Game Jam 2026](https://globalgamejam.org/games/2026/real6-8) by a team of 6 in Tokyo.

**Play it:** [globalgamejam.org/games/2026/real6-8](https://globalgamejam.org/games/2026/real6-8) | [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/petegilb/real6/releases)

---

## About

Built with Unreal Engine 5 at [Let's Games! Tokyo](https://globalgamejam.org/jam-sites/2025/lets-games-tokyo).

Steal masks from enemies to gain their powers, then use those powers to navigate through the level and reach the goal.

## Gameplay

- **Steal masks** from patrolling enemies by sneaking up and interacting with them
- Each enemy carries a different power:
  - **Carry** — pick up and move boxes
  - **Jump** — enhanced jumping
- Use the right power at the right time to get past obstacles
- Reach checkpoints to save your progress — getting caught sends you back

## Controls

|  Action  | Input |
|----------|-------|
| Move     |  WASD |
| Jump     | Space |
| Interact |   E   |
